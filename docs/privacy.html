Review Lens privacy policy

Effective 04|10|2026

Review Lens is a browser extension that summarises the reviews and comments on a web page into themes. It has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What data is used, and when

  • Page text. Only when you click the Review Lens icon (or press its shortcut), the extension reads the visible text of the current tab, or only the text you selected. On YouTube and Reddit it reads only the comments and the video or post title. It never reads pages on its own or in the background.
  • Your Gemini API key. You provide your own key. It is stored only in your browser (chrome.storage.local) and is sent only to Google's Gemini API to make requests.
  • Analysis results. Your last 20 analyses are stored only in your browser so you can compare them. Removing the extension deletes them.

Who receives it

The page text is sent directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API (generativelanguage.googleapis.com) using your key, and the result comes back to your browser. Nothing is sent to the developer or any other party. Google processes the request under the Gemini API Terms and Google Privacy Policy. Note that on Gemini's free tier, Google may use requests to improve its products, so avoid analysing pages with private information.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect, store, sell or share your data.
  • We don't track browsing history or use cookies.
  • We don't use the data for advertising, credit decisions or anything other than the analysis you requested.

Permissions

  • activeTab and scripting: read the current tab's text only after you click the icon.
  • storage: keep your key, settings and recent analyses in your browser.
  • generativelanguage.googleapis.com: send the analysis request to Gemini.
