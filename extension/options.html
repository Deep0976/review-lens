Review Lens settings

Review Lens uses Google's Gemini AI with your own free key. It takes about a minute:

  1. Open aistudio.google.com/apikey and sign in with Google.
  2. Click Create API key and copy it.
  3. Paste it below and click Save.

The free tier allows about 20 requests a day per model, and one analysis is one request. If you hit the limit, try another model, e.g. gemini-3-flash-preview.

Privacy: only when you click the icon, the page's visible text is sent to Google's Gemini API. Your key and results stay in this browser. Privacy policy

How to use: open any review page, Reddit thread or YouTube video (scroll to load comments first) and click the Review Lens icon, or press Alt+Shift+R.
