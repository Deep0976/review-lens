No sign-up needed. You get 5 free analyses every day.

How to use

  1. Open a review page, a Reddit thread or a YouTube video. On YouTube and Reddit, scroll down first so the comments load.
  2. Click the Review Lens icon in the toolbar, or press Alt+Shift+R. Tip: pin it from the puzzle-piece menu.
  3. Read the themes, the summary and the quotes. "Open on page" jumps to the original comment.
  4. Analysed two pages? Compare them side by side from any report.

Optional: unlimited use with your own key

Add a free Google Gemini key to skip the daily limit. Create one at aistudio.google.com/apikey (about a minute). Leave it empty to use the free daily analyses.
